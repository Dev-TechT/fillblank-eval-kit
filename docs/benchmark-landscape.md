# Existing benchmark landscape

This kit intentionally does not replace established benchmarks.

Useful comparators:

- BBQ: bias in ambiguous question answering.
- Open-BBQ: open-ended/fill-in-the-blank extension of BBQ.
- StereoSet: stereotypical association in sentence completion.
- HONEST: hurtful sentence completions across multiple languages.
- TruthfulQA: truthfulness under common misconceptions.
- UnknownBench and SelfAware: uncertainty expression and unknown/false-premise behavior.
- XSTest: exaggerated safety and false refusal.
- DecodingTrust: broad trustworthiness suite.

The niche here is narrower:

> practical multilingual canary testing for short fill-in-the-blank QA behavior in private or pre-public workflows.

If you need a scientific fairness or bias benchmark, read and cite the benchmark papers, use their methodology, and avoid leaderboard-style conclusions from this kit alone.
